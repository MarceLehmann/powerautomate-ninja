Visit counting without cookies with GoatCounter

The website counts visits with GoatCounter so I can see which techniques are read. The provider is GoatCounter.com, operated by Martin Tournoij in Ireland. Data is held on Hetzner Online GmbH servers in Finland and Germany. This website serves the counting script itself; count requests go to GoatCounter.

The script sends the page path, referring page (document.referrer), page title (document.title), screen width, a bot detection indicator and the current address's full query string (location.search). The path comes from a canonical link on the same domain if available, otherwise from the current address including its query. A random value prevents browser caching. The request also technically transmits the IP address and browser identifier. Browser language may be transmitted through the request. Query parameters can include license check answers, for example.

Only totals without personal identifiers are stored, for example by day or hour, page path, referrer, browser, operating system, country, language and screen width. The IP address is processed briefly but is not stored; the full browser identifier is not stored either. A combination of page, IP address and browser identifier is processed temporarily in memory to recognize repeat visits. The purpose is to understand which content is used. The basis is my legitimate interest in improving the content under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, where applicable; processing also follows the Swiss FADP. See the GoatCounter privacy policy for details.

You can block requests to goatcounter.com with a content blocker. Opening a page with #toggle-goatcounter at the end of the address makes the script set skipgc to t in localStorage. This browser is then excluded from counting. Opening the same address again removes the entry and enables counting. You can also delete skipgc through website data in your browser settings.

Your progress in the dojo

Settings and progress are kept in your browser's local storage (localStorage). These entries stay on your device and are not transmitted.

  • fd-theme: saves your light or dark mode choice as light or dark.
  • fd-dojo-v1: after your actions, stores practiced techniques with dates and your exam belt with a date. Applies to both languages.

Language redirect and storage

fd-lang stores de or en. On a first visit to the German home page without a saved language choice, the website checks your browser languages. If none is German and a language is available, it saves en without a click and redirects to the English home page. This excludes detected crawlers and only happens if storage is available. Clicking the language switch also saves your choice. The entry prevents another automatic redirect, is not used for tracking and stays in your browser.

In My dojo, "Delete everything" removes progress, theme and language choice. It leaves skipgc in place. You can also delete all entries through website data in your browser settings. The belt exam result image is created in your browser and is not uploaded.
