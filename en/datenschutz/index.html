    Privacy

    As of: 2026-10-16

    In short

    • This website sets no cookies.
    • No tracking and no analytics run on this website.
    • There are no accounts and no forms that send anything.
    • The website loads no fonts, scripts or images from third parties.
    • What you mark in the dojo or reach in the exam stays in your browser. I receive none of it.

    Responsible

    Marcel Lehmann (trade name KMUpower), reachable at marcel.lehmann@kmupower.com. Full details are in the legal notice.

    Hosting on GitHub Pages

    The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub Inc. in the USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary access data in server logs, for example your IP address and the time of access. I do not analyze these logs. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. Details are in the GitHub privacy statement.

    Your progress in the dojo

    Some settings and your progress are stored in your browser's local storage (localStorage). This is not a cookie. The entries stay on your device and are never sent to me or to third parties. Nothing is written until you do something.

    • fd-theme: "light" or "dark". Remembers light or dark mode.
    • fd-lang: "de" or "en". Remembers your language choice so the home page does not redirect you again. Set when you switch the language or when the home page redirects you to English once.
    • fd-dojo-v1: your practiced techniques with date and your belt from the exam with date. Applies to both languages.

    Deleting: under My dojo, "Delete everything" removes all entries. You can also delete them in your browser settings.

    The result image of the belt exam is created in your browser. It is never uploaded.

    If you write to me

    The link "Report an error in this question" only prepares an email in your own mail program. Nothing is sent until you click send there. I use your email address and the content only to reply and to correct the question, and I delete the email once that is done.

    Links to other websites

    This website links to powerplatformtip.com, Microsoft Learn, the Power Platform Academy, LinkedIn and other websites. Only when you click a link does the provider receive your data. Links to powerplatformtip.com and the Academy carry a source parameter in the address (utm_source=powerautomate.ninja). This website does not evaluate the parameter itself, the target website does so under its own privacy policy.

    Your rights

    Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection you can request information or deletion. The website itself does not store any personal data with me. For the hosting logs, contact GitHub. For everything else, write to me at marcel.lehmann@kmupower.com.

    Independent website, not affiliated with Microsoft.

    Questions and explanations on flows, based on the tips from powerplatformtip.com. No sign-up, no cookies, no tracking. Your progress stays in your browser.
