    About the dojo

    The PowerPlatformAcademy Dojo for Power Automate tests and explains what the tips on powerplatformtip.com show. One question after another, from the first test to architecture.

    How it works

    • Every technique starts with a quiz question. Guess first, then read: more sticks than with reading alone.
    • The solution explains every answer, including the wrong ones. Then follow a short explanation, the expression from the tip, when to use the technique and the most common mistake.
    • The step-by-step guide with screenshots is in the tip on powerplatformtip.com. Every technique links there.
    • The belt exam tells you where you stand. The license check tells you whether the license from Microsoft 365 is enough.

    Where the content comes from

    All questions come from the weekly tips by Marcel Lehmann on powerplatformtip.com. Only tips that are current after the last review make it into the dojo. License statements and limit numbers are based on Microsoft Learn, with a link on every page. If an explanation here is wrong, the tip is corrected first and the dojo after that.

    What the dojo is not

    Not a Microsoft offering, not a certification and no licensing advice for individual cases. The belts are a learning aid of this website.

    Part of the Power Platform Academy

    The PowerPlatformAcademy Dojo is part of the Power Platform Academy. There is a separate dojo for Copilot at copilot.ninja.

    Found an error?

    Every technique page has the link "Report an error in this question". Or write directly to marcel.lehmann@kmupower.com.
